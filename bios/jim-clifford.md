# Jim Clifford

**Short (one line, for a programme or introduction):**
Jim Clifford is a Professor of History at the University of Saskatchewan who uses computing to read historical records at scale, and co-edits the journal *Historical Methods*.

**Brief bio (about 120 words):**
Jim Clifford is a Professor of History at the University of Saskatchewan and co-editor of *Historical Methods*, a journal of quantitative and digital history. At the university's Historical GIS Lab he builds spatial databases and text-mining pipelines for environmental and imperial history. His book *West Ham and the River Lea: A Social and Environmental History of London's Industrialized Marshland, 1839–1914* (UBC Press, 2017) used digitised survey maps to trace how industry and pollution reshaped a London suburb. His current Imperial Careers project read a century of the *Colonial Office List* and the *India Office List* to map the careers of tens of thousands of officials, using open language models on desktop GPUs and the Digital Research Alliance of Canada's clusters. With Jo Guldi, he also co-edits *Working Papers in Critical Search*.
