# Jim Clifford

**Short (one line, for a programme or introduction):**
Jim Clifford is a historian at the University of Saskatchewan who uses computing to read historical records at scale, and co-edits the journal *Historical Methods*.

**Brief bio (about 120 words):**
Jim Clifford is Associate Professor of Environmental History at the University of Saskatchewan, where he directs the Historical GIS Lab. His book *West Ham and the River Lea* (UBC Press, 2017) used digitised nineteenth-century survey maps to trace how industry, pollution and water shortages reshaped a London suburb. His current work turns printed sources into structured data: the Imperial Careers project read a century of the *Colonial Office List* and the *India Office List* to map the careers of tens of thousands of officials, using open language models on desktop GPUs and the Digital Research Alliance of Canada's clusters. He co-edits *Historical Methods*, a journal of quantitative and digital history, and, with Jo Guldi, *Working Papers in Critical Search*.
